The archive contains the complete derivative road database as SQLite, including
road names, OSM way and node identifiers, geometry, direction and explicit speed
limits. No offline basemap, place-search index, routing engine or live incident
feed is included. Conditional, variable, lane-specific and symbolic speed limits
are withheld. No speed limit is inferred from road class. Follow posted signs.
